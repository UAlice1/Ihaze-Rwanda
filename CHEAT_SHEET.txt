❓ Q&A RESPONSES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: "Data from 2016-2017 is too old?"
A: "Framework works with any NISR survey. We'd update with 2024 
   data before deployment. Methodology is what matters, not 
   specific year."

Q: "Why not machine learning predictions?"
A: "Officers trust 'who reported problems' more than 'who we 
   predict will have problems.' Current needs are actionable now. 
   We could add ML later with validated training data."

Q: "Better than Excel how?"
A: "Automatic data validation, barrier-specific matching, capacity 
   planning scenarios. Officers don't need statistical expertise."

Q: "Data privacy?"
A: "Only aggregate district statistics are public. No individual 
   household data in app or GitHub. AI only sees aggregates."

Q: "Scale to other countries?"
A: "Yes. Framework is universal. Specific indicators adapt to 
   local surveys. Rwanda is pilot; vision is regional."
