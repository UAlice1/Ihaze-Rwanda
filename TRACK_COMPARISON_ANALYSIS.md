## 🚨 Devil's Advocate: Could You Lose with Agriculture?

### Scenarios Where Agriculture Track Could Fail:

1. **"Data is too old"** objection
   - **Mitigation:** Emphasize methodology is reusable with newer data; focus on the analytical framework, not 2016-2017 specifics

2. **"Too narrow focus"** (only soil erosion)
   - **Mitigation:** Show that your framework applies to ANY barrier analysis (water, credit, markets); erosion is just the demonstration case

3. **"Other teams have fancier AI"**
   - **Mitigation:** Position transparency as a feature, not a bug; government doesn't want black boxes

4. **"Prediction vs. description"**
   - **Mitigation:** "We find who needs help NOW, not who MIGHT need help LATER. That's more actionable."

5. **"Limited scalability"**
   - **Mitigation:** Show how it integrates with NISR data pipelines; works for any new survey

### Your Counter-Strategy:
Focus on **responsible AI**, **government trust**, and **operational readiness**. You're not selling a demo—you're selling a tool extension officers can use Monday morning.
